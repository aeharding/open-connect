Preconditions for COMPLETE-disable:
- Valid internally consistent pending DISABLE intent: prior stable candidate,
  desired original; validated stored original/candidate/journal/profile.
- Explicit opt-in; default recover remains ABORT. Never silently adopt candidate
  without journal or complete an ENABLE through this capability.
- Recheck actual image before any transition; unknown edit/metadata/link, corrupt
  or missing evidence is a conflict. No guessed restore or fallback to external master.
- Success means exact original plus a recorded model commit to disabled/no pending;
  original bytes alone are insufficient. An interruption BEFORE final commit keeps
  pending intent; the next caller explicitly chooses ABORT or COMPLETE-disable.
  A verified committed-disabled state is complete and may be a read-only no-op.
- Already committed disabled with no pending work may be a read-only verified no-op;
  no-pending enabled is not an implicit uninstall. Exact API behavior belongs to t12.
