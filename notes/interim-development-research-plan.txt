5. VERIFIED MAZDA-AUTHORED RETROFIT PREREQUISITE AND LIMITS
Primary source: Mazda CSP02 / retrofit installation packet, NHTSA mirror https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2018/MC-10144323-9999.pdf
PDF page 9/20, printed Common-2, specifies v70.00.21 or later before hub installation and warns older software may not recognize the hub.
The requirement repeats on PDF page 13/20, printed MAZDA6-1; the packet identifies TK78 66 9U0** hubs and C922-V6-605 cabling.
Corroboration: Mazda SA-001/19, issued 2019-04-05, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2019/MC-10159198-0001.pdf
Printed/PDF page 3/9 describes failed new-hub recognition blocking USB software updates; pages 7-9 describe original-hub/cable recovery.
Page 9/9 names 70.00.021B or later. Preserve the documents' literal version forms rather than silently equating patch requirements.
Inference: installed 55.00.750B is below this retrofit prerequisite; a CarPlay hub is not a supported CarPlay baseline on the present firmware.
This does not prove all USB/SD functions fail, identify this bench's exact hub revision, or justify a flash. Those remain separate evidence gaps.
Recovery described in the bulletin entails hardware changes and is a dependency, not an instruction to the user or an action authorized here.
Before any later upgrade proposal: preserve baseline, establish independent access/recovery, identify hub/region, verify exact update path and packages, and document rollback limits.
Research backlog: actual hub identity and legacy USB/SD behavior; complete recovery image/restore feasibility; boot/power sequencing; Ethernet chipset/driver/SSH support.
